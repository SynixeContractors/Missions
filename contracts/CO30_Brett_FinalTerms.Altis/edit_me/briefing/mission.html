A major economic agreement is set to be discussed between Altis and the IEA trade bloc, with representatives from government, industry, and logistics expected to attend formal negotiations in the capital. The proposed deal has sparked significant opposition from right-wing nationalist groups, who view the agreement as a threat to sovereignty, local industry, and national identity. A senior executive from a major multinational corporation has been invited to advise the delegation on trade logistics, infrastructure, and long-term investment planning. Aster Holdings has contracted Synixe to provide protection for their executive, Arkady Prokofyev, during transit, arrival, and the initial phase of the negotiations. The executive is scheduled to arrive at the airfield at 0500. Synixe are to meet the arrival, secure the executive, and transport him to a secure hotel for the morning. At 0900, the executive is due to move from the hotel to the government office in Pyrgos for coordination with the delegation and local officials. The company has been briefed on heightened political risk and is expected to remain in contact with local authorities while movement and route security are assessed. Intelligence suggests that hostile elements may attempt to disrupt the delegation, intimidate participants, or create a security incident before talks begin. Synixe personnel are expected to provide a discreet but effective security presence, secure the executive's movement between the airfield, hotel, and government office, and keep the client safe while the political climate remains unstable. The operation is intended to be low-profile unless hostile action escalates. Contractors are expected to maintain professional restraint, prioritise the safety of the executive and surrounding personnel, and avoid unnecessary confrontation with local security forces, civilians, or political demonstrators.
