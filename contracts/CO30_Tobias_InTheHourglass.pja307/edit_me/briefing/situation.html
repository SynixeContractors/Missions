EMPLOYER Name: Ngabiri Security Brigade - Dariyah Province Location: South-Western Airfield and Diminli Camp The Ngabiri Security Brigade is responsible for maintaining security in the region and originally contracted Synixe's Charlie Team to transport vital supplies to the south-western airfield. Ngabiri forces used to maintain a larger garrison in the region, but have since reduced their presence. Because of this, many of their old camps and airfields have been abandoned. Their northern airfield was abandoned after a haboob destroyed several aircraft and other critical infrastructure. ENEMY FORCES Name: Unknown FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Synixe Contractors - Charlie Team Charlie team's convoy is missing, and they have not been heard from in several hours. They were tasked with delivering a shipment of supplies to a remote airfield in south-western Dariyah, but have not arrived. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Name: The Ngabiri People The Ngabiri people are present, but they are not expected to be a significant factor in the operation. Contractors should avoid unnecessary engagement with civilians and minimize collateral damage.
