Virohlati, a mainland province of the Kingdom of Nyrland, has seen a sharp escalation in violence. What began as rising hostility toward civil servants in the town of Virojoki culminated in the disappearance and murder of ten off‑duty police officers, followed by a car bombing outside the police station that killed seven and injured ten. Days later, Police Chief Victor Schild was found murdered in his home. Writing left at the scene in both cases points to the extremist group known as the Sons of the Coming Dawn and the figure called the Red Rider, responsible for severe harm and loss of life. Moments ago, an active shooter incident was reported at Mercy‑West Hospital. Synixe has been tasked with responding to the hospital first, then continuing the investigation to identify and neutralize hostile elements and prevent further attacks.
