Virojoki has seen a rise in targeted violence against civil servants, culminating in the disappearance and murder of ten off‑duty police officers, a bombing at the police station, and the killing of Police Chief Victor Schild. Writing left at the scene points to the Sons of the Coming Dawn and the figure known as the Red Rider. Moments ago, an active shooter incident was reported at Mercy‑West Hospital. Synixe has been tasked with responding to the hospital first, before continuing their investigation to identify and neutralize hostile elements and prevent further violence.
