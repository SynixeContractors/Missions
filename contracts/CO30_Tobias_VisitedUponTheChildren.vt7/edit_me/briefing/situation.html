EMPLOYER Name: The Kingdom of Nyrland Location: Virohlati Virohlati is a mainland province of the Kingdom of Nyrland. Its long coastline and numerous islands make it a frequent corridor for trafficking illicit goods, weapons, and people. Recently, the town of Virojoki has seen a sharp rise in targeted violence against civil servants. ENEMY FORCES Name: Sons of the Coming Dawn Location: Virohlati The Sons of the Coming Dawn are an apocalyptic cult convinced they must trigger a catastrophic “new dawn,” using coercion, violence, and prophetic leadership to force the world toward the collapse they believe is destined. Their local chapter is led by the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. The "Red Rider," Grand Wizard Elias Brandt - is believed to be responsible for the attacks. FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Royal Nyrlander Defense Forces Location: Virohlati Friendly forces have set up a Forward Operating Base at Virojoki Airport. There is a small garrison south-east of Virojoki at a small communications outpost. Name: Gendarmerie Location: Virohlati The Gendarmerie is responsible for policing duties across the province, including security at public events and the regional hospital. Its local precinct is severely understaffed following the deaths of seventeen officers, the murder of its chief, and injuries sustained by ten more. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Name: Civilians Location: Virojoki The citizens of Virojoki have been shaken by the surge in extreme violence but remain determined to continue daily life. Public events are still ongoing, and civilians will be present throughout the town.
