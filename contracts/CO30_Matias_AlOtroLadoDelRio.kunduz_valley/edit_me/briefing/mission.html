For the last three months, Synixe’s Charlie Team has been training the Tura militia under Virelian orders so that they can eventually overthrow the Tiyen forces that currently control the region. The time has finally arrived for the Tura to take the fight to the Tiyen forces and kick them out of the region, following a Tura mortar strike that destroyed the Tiyen base closest to the river. Unfortunately, Charlie Team had to be pulled away for another operation, and we are being brought in to aid the Tura. Contractors must move into enemy territory and destroy the Lugopa Airfield and a mortar base. Additionally, we have been tasked with capturing or killing Xien Guao, the man in charge of Tiyen operations in the region. Tura forces will initiate an assault on the southern Tiyen bases at 0600, with the objective of destroying them as well. Virelian intelligence has revealed that Guao keeps a contingent of Tiyen special forces as his security detail.
