The Hööpakka hunting club has been under observation by the local police for years due to its unusual initiation customs, such as ritualistically drinking the blood of fallen prey. However, the true scope wasn't known until last week when the group attacked a soap factory west of Troihari. Police sent to the site were subsequently fired on with machine guns, suffering heavy casualties. None of the 11 shift workers at the site have been accounted for. From eyewitness testimony of the evacuated civilians, the group is led by Krasnovian special forces and has taken multiple structures north of Hööpakka. According to immigration records, there were 5 Krasnovian citizens who entered the country via a nearby airport during the week before the attacks.
