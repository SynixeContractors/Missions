Hööpakka hunting club has been under the watch from the local police for years due their unusual initiation customs like ritualistic drinking blood of their fallen pray. However the true scope wasn't known until last week when the group attacked soap factory west of Troihari. Police sent to the site has been subsequently shot by machinguns taking many casualties. Noone from the 11 shift workers at the site has been accounted for. From eyewitness testimony of the evacuated civilians, the group is led by Krasnovian special forces and have taken multiple structures north of Hööpakka. According to immigration records, there were 5 Krasnovian citizens who entered the country via a nearby airport during the week before the attacks.
