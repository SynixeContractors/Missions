In their quest to usher in the apocalypse, the Sons of the Coming Dawn have established a base of operations on the island of Säkkiluoto. Its approximately 20 residents have gone missing, and their current status is unknown. In an attempt to broker a deal with the insurgents, Suursari Governor Johnathan Ward traveled to Säkkiluoto by helicopter with his Chief of Staff Matthew Montgomery and Political Advisor Silas Mercer. Shortly after landing, insurgents subdued the Governor’s security team and took the Governor and his staff hostage. Synixe has been tasked with the rescue of: - Governor Johnathan Ward - Chief of Staff Matthew Montgomery - Political Advisor Silas Mercer - The pilot and co‑pilot - 2‑man security team
