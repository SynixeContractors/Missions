EMPLOYER Name: The Kingdom of Nyrland Location: Suursari The Suursari archipelago is a semi-autonomous protectorate of the Kingdom of Nyrland. Due to its remote and secluded location, it has seen an uptick in insurgent activity. The Kingdom of Nyrland has hired Synixe to rescue the Suursari Governor, his staff and any civilians trapped on the island of Säkkiluoto. ENEMY FORCES Name: Sons of the Coming Dawn Location: Säkkiluoto The Sons of the Coming Dawn are an apocalyptic cult convinced they must trigger a catastrophic “new dawn,” using coercion, violence, and prophetic leadership to force the world toward the collapse they believe is destined. Their local chapter is led by the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. The "White Rider," Grand Wizard Patrick Craymer - who is believed to be on the island. FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Royal Nyrlander Defense Forces - NATRES Location: Suurkylä Some members of the Royal Nyrlander Defense Forces National Reserve have provided support to Synixe. They are unequipped to provide operational assistance. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Name: Civilians Location: Säkkiluoto Säkkiluoto is home to roughly 20 permanent residents. Their current status is unknown.
