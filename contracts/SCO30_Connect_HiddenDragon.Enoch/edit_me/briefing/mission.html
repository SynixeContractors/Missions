Synixe has been contracted to disrupt NGLASW program of the client's competitor. The missile designed by LGZ, nicknamed "Dragon", is currently undergoing live-fire testing. The missile is dropped from a Livonian Defense Force aircraft onto a range. Due to increased threat, the program is being run under strict secrecy. The test articles are shipped by train to Topolin, and then taken by truck to previously abandoned Lukow Airfield. The program's lead engineer, Zenon Jabłoński, is local to the area and lives somewhere in Radacz. Due to threats on his life, he keeps a significant personal detail at his house.
