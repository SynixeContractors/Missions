Kujari has been a hotbed for illicit smuggling operations for years, and the Ngabiri National Police (NNP) have been struggling to contain the situation. The Swift Response Battalion (SRB), a splinter faction of the Ngabiri Security Brigade, has been responsible for several attacks against the NNP and is known to be heavily armed and hostile to any outside forces. Contractors from Synixe have been called in to assist the NNP in tracking and eliminating the SRB's smuggling operations. Intelligence is expecting at least three weapons shipments to be smuggled into Kujari Province by the SRB. Contractors are tasked with locating all three dropzones, tracking the shipments to their final destination, and destroying the weapons before they can be used against the government.
