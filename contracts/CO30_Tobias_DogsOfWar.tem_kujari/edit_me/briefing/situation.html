EMPLOYER Name: Ngabiri National Police - Kujari Province Location: Marwey The Ngabiri National Police (NNP) have long suspected Kujari to be a hub for illicit smuggling operations. The NNP has requested the assistance of Synixe Contractors to track and eliminate a smuggling operation run by the Swift Response Battalion (SRB). ENEMY FORCES Name: Swift Response Battalion The SRB is a well-armed and highly trained paramilitary organization. The SRB has been smuggling weapons and supplies into Kujari Province, and they are known to be hostile to any outside forces. Contractors should expect heavy resistance when engaging the SRB. Because of the operation's low-profile nature, the SRB is not expected to employ heavy weapons or armored vehicles. FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Ngabiri National Police The NNP has several checkpoint and presence patrols in the area, but they are not expected to engage the SRB directly. It is important to note that corruption is rampant within the NNP, and some officers may be sympathetic to the SRB. Contractors should exercise caution when interacting with NNP personnel. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Name: The Ngabiri People The Ngabiri people are present, but they are not expected to be a significant factor in the operation. Contractors should avoid unnecessary engagement with civilians and minimize collateral damage. Name: IDAP IDAP is a humanitarian organization that operates in the region. Contractors should avoid interfering with IDAP operations and respect their presence.
