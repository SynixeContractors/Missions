Yesterday the separatist group stormed The Church of the Sacred Bloody Viscera of the Body of Christ Hung Upon the Cross and took the clergy and visitors hostage. The group was previously flagged by the organized crime unit, but the leads always led to dead ends. They were deemed low priority until now. The client believes the group got support from outside the island. Police approaching the monstery have been shot by heavy machineguns, and with two policeman gravely wounded, they have retreated and blocked the roads leading into the town. According to the interviews of fleeing populace the gunmen approached monstary in a vehicle convoy from the south west. SWAT team from Corazol attempted during the night to breach the fence on the eastern side, but were stopped by placed anti-personel mines which costed life of three policeman.
