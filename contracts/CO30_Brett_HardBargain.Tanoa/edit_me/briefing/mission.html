Mercy Tide's six staff were taken from their clinic when Syndikat moved in: Doctor Hanna Weiss, Doctor Samuel Okafor and four Tanoan nurses and drivers, Mele Tavita, Iosefa Kauhalo, Ana Lealofi and Tevita Naufahu. They are believed to be held inside houses in the marked villages, but not which houses or which villages. They may have been split up. Syndikat has tied the hostages' hands. Contractors should leave them tied and escort them out, as they will be in no state to move quickly or quietly on their own. Bring them to the Volunteer Guard checkpoint, where the guards will take over. Solomone Mahe is believed to be in one of the three villages. Capturing or killing him is a secondary objective, and would make Syndikat think twice before taking aid workers again. He is not a fighter and is expected to run for a vehicle as soon as his village is attacked. Syndikat likely course of action: bandits garrisoning houses and holding the centre of each village, with patrols on foot around them, roadblocks on the roads in, and jeeps driving between the villages. Heavy machine guns may be set up in the villages. Once a village is attacked, the other Syndikat groups nearby will move to help, and paramilitary technicals and pickups will come in from outside the area.
