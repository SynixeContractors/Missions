Syndikat runs smuggling, extortion and protection rackets across rural Tanoa, where the Horizon Islands government has little reach. Two days ago Syndikat fighters moved into three villages, marked on the map, and took six staff of a mobile medical clinic working in the area. Most residents left the same day. Yesterday Syndikat sent a ransom demand with a video of the hostages. EMPLOYER Name: Mercy Tide Medical Relief Location: Virelia, with clinics across the Horizon Islands A Virelian medical charity running mobile clinics in rural Tanoa. Mercy Tide will not pay the ransom and, with the Horizon Islands government's agreement, has contracted Synixe to recover its staff alive. ENEMY FORCES Name: Syndikat Location: The three marked villages and the roads between them Around a hundred armed men. Most are local bandits with mixed rifles, light machine guns and some launchers, holding the villages and roadblocks and driving jeeps between them. Syndikat's better equipped paramilitary wing has technicals with heavy machine guns and anti-tank launchers, and more of them will come in if the villages are attacked. The kidnapping is run by Solomone Mahe, a Syndikat lieutenant with a paramilitary bodyguard. INDEPENDENT FORCES Name: Horizon Islands Volunteer Guard Location: Checkpoint marked on the map A small team at the checkpoint will take over the hostages, untie them and give them medical care. They will not leave the checkpoint. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Name: Village residents Location: The three marked villages Nearly all residents have left. Treat anyone unarmed as a civilian or a hostage until proven otherwise.
