Syndikat has run smuggling, extortion and protection rackets across rural Tanoa for years. The Horizon Islands government has little reach outside the towns, and in parts of the interior Syndikat is the closest thing there is to a local authority. Two days ago Syndikat fighters moved into three villages, which are marked on the map, and took six staff of a mobile medical clinic that was working in the area. Most residents left the same day. Yesterday Syndikat sent Mercy Tide a ransom demand with a video of the hostages. EMPLOYER Name: Mercy Tide Medical Relief Location: Virelia, with clinics across the Horizon Islands A Virelian medical charity running mobile clinics in rural Tanoa. Mercy Tide will not pay the ransom. With the Horizon Islands government's agreement, it has contracted Synixe to recover its six staff alive. ENEMY FORCES Name: Syndikat Location: The three marked villages and the roads between them Around a hundred armed men. Most are local bandits with mixed rifles, light machine guns and some launchers, holding the villages, roadblocks and the roads between them in jeeps. Syndikat's paramilitary wing is better equipped and drives technicals with heavy machine guns and anti-tank launchers. More of them are outside the area and will come in if the villages are attacked. The kidnapping is run by Solomone Mahe, a Syndikat lieutenant. He is protected by a paramilitary bodyguard. FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Synixe Contractors None besides Synixe. INDEPENDENT FORCES Name: Horizon Islands Volunteer Guard Location: Checkpoint marked on the map The Volunteer Guard does not have the training or equipment to go into the villages. A small team at the marked checkpoint will take over the hostages, untie them and give them medical care. They will not leave the checkpoint. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Name: Village residents Location: The three marked villages Nearly all residents left two days ago. Anyone unarmed should be treated as a civilian or a hostage until proven otherwise.
