Having driven out most of the resistance out of Anizay Basin, Khaybaran forces have been redeployed elsewhere. Now the government forces have to push them out of the hills in the south. Recent reports suggest there is small unit of Livionian special forces advising and conducting electronic warfare out of the hills. A patrol lead by Virelian advisor has been ambushed south of Gorgan and he is currently held hostage somewhere in Qalandar. Captured LLF member has divulged information that there are two seperate livionian EW operations run from the area around Gorgan. He claims he has never seen them in Gorqan proper, only passing by the main road through town.
