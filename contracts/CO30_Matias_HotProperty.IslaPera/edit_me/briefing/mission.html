For years, the Buena Fortuna Resort has operated on the island southwest of Isla Pera without incident, despite sharing the island with the Desgraciados cartel. This arrangement was maintained through protection payments to the cartel, along with an understanding that the continued operation of the resort was beneficial to Isla Pera. However, a shift in leadership within the cartel placed Manuel Galindo, a violent and hot-headed individual, in charge. One of Galindo’s first orders was for his men operating the cartel camps on the island to raid the resort for valuables. This resulted in a violent robbery, with the loss of life among resort security, guests, and cartel members. As a result, resort operations have been suspended for the time being while an investigation takes place. The resort’s parent company has hired Synixe to enter cartel territory and recover the stolen property so that it can be returned to the guests and the resort. The company has also been in contact with local law enforcement and reached an agreement allowing Synixe contractors, if possible, to execute a kill-or-capture order on Galindo should they encounter information pointing to his whereabouts. The stolen valuables include handheld electronics, watches, and large amounts of cash taken from the hotel’s safe. Additionally, the cartel stole a weapons crate belonging to the hotel’s security team. The employer has authorized Synixe to use vehicles from the island’s vehicle pool for the operation. Contractors are free to select whichever vehicle they deem appropriate.
