For years the Buena Fortuna resort has operated in the island South West of Isla Pera with no issues, despite sharing the island with the Desgraciados cartel. This had been achieved through protection payments to the cartel and the understanding that the continued operation of the resort was benefitial for Isla Pera. However a shift in leadership in the cartel positioned Manuel Galindo, a violent and hot-headed individual, as the new boss. One of Galindo's first orders was for his men that operate the cartel camps in the island to raid the resort for valuables. This resulted in a violent robbery with loss of life of both security, guests and cartel members. As a result, resort operations are suspended for the time being while an investigation transpires. The resort's parent company has hired Synixe to move into cartel territory and recover the stolen property so that it can be returned to the guests and the resort. They have also talked with local law enforcement and have reached an agreement that allows Synixe to if possible, execute a kill or capture order on Galindo, should contractors encounter information pointing to his whereabouts.
