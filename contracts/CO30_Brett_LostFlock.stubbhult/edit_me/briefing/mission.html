The Covenant of the Last Dawn (CLD) is an isolated religious movement led by Michał Zawadzki. The CLD believes that the outside world has become corrupted by modern society and that a great reset, brought about through divine intervention, is approaching. Drawing comparisons to the great flood described in religious texts, they believe that only those who remain faithful and prepared will survive the coming catastrophe. The group's armed wing, known as the Wardens, was originally established to provide security for CLD sites. Over time, however, it has developed into a capable and increasingly well-trained paramilitary force. Nine members of the Covenant recently attempted to leave the organization. They were not permitted to do so. Zawadzki ordered the group detained, fearing they would disclose information regarding the Wardens' training, organization, and capabilities to law enforcement. One of the nine has since escaped and provided authorities with critical intelligence. The remaining eight are believed to still be held by the Covenant. The escapee identified four locations used by the Wardens within the forest: three smaller outposts and one larger site known as "The Keep." The outposts are believed to be relatively small and lightly defended, while The Keep is more heavily fortified and serves as the central hub for the Wardens' operations. We do not know the exact locations of these sites, nor which location could currently be holding the remaining eight detainees. Following the revelation of the CLD's activities, the government has issued a $10,000 reward for information leading to Zawadzki's arrest, and a $250,000 reward for his successful apprehension.
