With the Arganan civil war in full swing, one group of workers that was unable to retreat to the designated rally point has been captured by advancing Arganan forces. Their return was negotiated with the Arganan military; however, on the way from Garmsar, their Arganan escort was ambushed. Arganan officials, blaming the negotiators for leaking information to the insurgency, have cut contact. Synixe was originally hired for protection in a different area, but with the current events in mind, Synixe has been ordered to rescue all 5 of the captives and bring them to safety. There is very little information on the current whereabouts of the captives. Heavy resistance is expected from both sides on the frontline.
