With Arganan civil war in full swing, one group of workers that was unable to retreat to designated rally point have been captured by advancing Arganan forces. Their return was negotiated with the Arganan military, however on the way from Garmsar, their Arganan escort was ambushed. Arganan officials blaming the negotiators for leaking information to the insurgency have cut contact. Synixe was originally hired for protection in different area but with the events advaincing has been ordered to bring all 5 of them to insertion point or humanitarian corridor. There is very little information on current whereabouts of the captives. Heavy resistance is expected from both sides on the frontline.
