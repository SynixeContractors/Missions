The last stage of the Treidan withdrawal is in effect. Remaining forces consist only of special forces training up the incoming rotation of PMCs to be able to perform minor incursions into the mountains. After the decommissioning of Firebase Hellion, rumors of significant stockpiles of equipment in close-to-running condition - that hadn't been properly decommissioned and instead were taken over by IaS - have spread like wildfire, no doubt fueled by boastful recruitment pictures from current local leader Ibrahim Uma. He additionally claimed that Farabad is no longer going to be safe this week. While denied by the Treidan government, the newest picture from yesterday may be a cause for concern. As part of a show-of-force strategy, contractors, once landed, will conduct offensive operations against the recently abandoned - now in the hands of IaS - Firebase Hellion. Once cleared, they are to move to the northeast and find the ammo cache that IaS has recently moved to support their current approach. Plane trasnporting Synixe contractors is holding supplies for the increased presence mission are to be delivered to ION's base Raven.
