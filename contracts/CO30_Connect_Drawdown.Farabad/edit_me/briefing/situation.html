EMPLOYER Name: Treidan Ministry of Defense Wanting to slow the bleed in public opinion at home, they decided to move toward a majority contractor-based approach in the areas around Farabad, as well as reduce their footprint. They have withdrawn from many positions, with contractors taking over only strategic and well-defensible posts. ENEMY FORCES Name: Ikhwan as-Sayf A militia group led by Ibrahim Uma following a drone strike on his father three months ago. They have captured a significant amount of territory during the withdrawal of the main Treidan forces. They have also been boasting about capturing large amounts of equipment from the abandoned camps—claims that have been denied by Treidan. FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Various contractors Location: Positions around the airfield and Farabad Holding defensive posture, they will not assist contractors in operations further north. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS Areas to the north have self-evacuated after withdrawal of Treidan peacekeepers.
