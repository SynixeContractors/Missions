EMPLOYER Name: Khaybara Government The government has agreed to the ceasefire and is withdrawing forces north of the river in order to pursue negotiations. Khaybara is interested in the safe extraction of IDAP personnel and the recovery of critical records that could compromise negotiations or reveal political and military information. ENEMY FORCES Name: Hostile Militias Location: South of the River These hostile groups are not formal military units, but are armed and organised enough to intimidate, blockade, and threaten IDAP personnel. They are motivated by anti-government sentiment and hostility toward humanitarian groups they believe are aligned with Khaybara. FRIENDLY FORCES Name: Khaybara Military Location: North of the River Friendly government forces are not expected to provide direct support. Their primary role is to maintain the northern side of the agreement and prepare for follow-on security operations after the extraction. INDEPENDENT FORCES Name: Southern Independence Movement Location: South of the River The Southern Independence Movement has granted limited permission for us to move in their areas of control. The SIM is not hostile so long as operations remain within the agreed window and do not appear to threaten their interests. Contractors are expected to follow instructions while in the area and to avoid unnecessary escalation. CIVILIAN CONSIDERATIONS The local civilian population is mixed in sentiment and may be fearful, uneasy, or hostile toward IDAP staff. Some residents see the relief workers as legitimate humanitarian actors, while others view them as government-aligned and therefore a threat. Contractors should avoid towns and populated routes when possible and use warning shots before escalating force when feasible.
