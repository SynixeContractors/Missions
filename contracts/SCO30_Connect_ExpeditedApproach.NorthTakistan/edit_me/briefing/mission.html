N'Gabirese ministry of interior has received credibile information about Illanzian support for IaS forces in the region. Two internationally wanted terrorist responsible for the capital metro attacks two weeks ago are cooperating with Illanzian forces to limit their casualties and expand their influence. Wanting to retain diplomatic option alongside significant additional deployment of armed forces to the region, N'Gabirese President has issued ultimatum for Illanzian forces to leave the region. That ultimatum expired yesterday and N'Gabirese army is ready to go on the offensive. The attack is scheduled for 11:30 local time. Synixe is to support the main advance, gathering information on the terrorist leaders and neutralize their threat.
