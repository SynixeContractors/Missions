Global Tetrahedron, on orders from an unknown client, has brought down the electrical grid in the Livonian province of Rucha on Sunday 28th of October. The investigation is being led by the electrical company Energia sp. z o.o., and the client believes Energia now possesses information critical to the case. Synixe is to lead a strike team into the country and find out what Energia knows. Locations of interest to the case include: the office building of Energia in Kaaranmännikkö, CTO's house in Rucha and a small server room in Kortesoja.
